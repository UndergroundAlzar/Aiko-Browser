Bundled filter snapshots and public suffix data

The GZIP files in this directory contain the unmodified text distributed by
their respective upstreams. The files are separate data consumed by Aiko's
own rule matcher. The corresponding text is included inside this APK and
in the source archive.

- EasyList (version 202611201235) and EasyPrivacy (version 202611201244)
  The EasyList authors (https://easylist.to/).
  GPL-3.0-or-later option per https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html.
  https://easylist.to/easylist/easylist.txt
  https://easylist.to/easylist/easyprivacy.txt
- AdGuard Chinese filter (version 2.1.69.90), including EasyList China
  https://filters.adtidy.org/extension/chromium/filters/224.txt
  Upstream license notices: https://github.com/AdguardTeam/AdguardFilters
